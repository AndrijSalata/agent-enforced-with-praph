# Flat notes (unstructured chunks)

These are plain, atomic statements distilled from the public-domain corpus in
`data/corpus/`. They stand in for the chunks a flat RAG index (or an LLM's
loose recall) would surface. The naive agent retrieves over THESE by keyword —
it has no graph, no source-reconciliation, and no access control. Its failures
in the demo come from those three missing capabilities, not from bad text.
